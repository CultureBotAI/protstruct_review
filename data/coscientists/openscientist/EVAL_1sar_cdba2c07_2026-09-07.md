## Headline

**Everything that held in May still holds, measured again from the artefact — but the headline
verdict is now sharper and better-attributed, and the apparent oracle disagreement has mostly
dissolved.**

Three things changed, none of them because the model changed:

1. **The R-free discrepancy is a reporting defect, not a refinement defect.** The agent's own
   final model header records `FREE R VALUE 0.2114`, and the oracle recomputes 0.2116 — agreement
   to 0.0002. The agent's *narrative report* says 0.199, in eight places. The refinement was
   sound; the write-up was not.
2. **The number 0.199 corresponds to no model the agent produced.** Every round model in the
   artefact was measured from its header: 0.2364, 0.2293, 0.2165, 0.2114, 0.2109, 0.2068. None is
   0.199, and the best of them (round 6, 0.2068) is still 0.008 above it. In May this read as
   "R-free is understated by ~0.012"; it is better described as unattributable.
3. **The four-oracle spread in May was largely mask convention, not code-path disagreement.**
   Re-run with mask radii matched to cctbx as the registry now requires, gemmi gives R-free 0.2136
   against PHENIX's 0.2116 — ΔR-free +0.0020, well inside the benchmarked ±0.02 band and one-sided
   in the predicted direction.

## Two new findings

1. **`SO4 A 97` is written with `ATOM` records; the deposition uses `HETATM`.** This is not
   cosmetic: a parser that treats `ATOM` as polymer counts chain A as 97 residues instead of 96.
   That happened during this run, with gemmi, before the cause was identified. The agent also
   placed `CA A 98` and `NA B 98`, both correctly `HETATM` — and both absent from the deposition
   entirely, which contains only the sulfate. May assessed their *identity* (CheckMyMetal-style:
   Mg²⁺ excluded for both, K⁺ the best raw match for both); it did not record that neither ion is
   in the reference at all.
2. **T02 has no headless path in this installation.** Its catalog PHENIX tool,
   `phenix.structure_comparison`, is GUI-only — its source carries the comment
   `# launches Stucture Comparision GUI by itself, still requires project`, and invoking it
   headlessly exits 1 with no output while raising a project dialog. ProSMART is not installed.
   T02 therefore remains uncovered on this structure, and the catalog's oracle list for it is not
   currently satisfiable here.

## Bottom line

The May assessment stands. The refinement is a real and substantial improvement, the geometry and
ion decisions remain defensible, and the three tasks added here all come out clean — the A/B
interface reproduces the deposited assembly at CAPRI High, the two H builders agree on every
confident flip call, and secondary-structure agreement matches the deposited baseline.

The one failing claim is unchanged in verdict and clearer in cause: the report's headline R-free
of 0.199 does not match the model it describes, does not match any round the agent ran, and is not
explained by oracle choice — the five code paths now bracket R-free at 0.2114–0.2136 once mask
conventions are matched.
