pass, or an independent verification of the agent's reported final model. No PHENIX,

## 5. Scientific interpretations withdrawn

These are corrections to claims, not discoveries of newly measured defects in
the absent reported-final model.

- **ProSMART is not RSRZ or a Ramachandran classifier (#692).** The reported
  local Procrustes score 0.5262 cannot occupy `T05_per_residue_rsrz`, use the
  unregistered 0.45 cutoff, or independently confirm Asn A39's Ramachandran
  classification. The old scalar is withdrawn. The historical Asn A39 flag
  remains explicitly attributed to cctbx validation, without invented
  independent confirmation or a comparison with round 7's zero-outlier claim.
- **The local metal screen is not canonical CheckMyMetal (#693).** Four
  candidate-distance scores retain their historical numbers (Ca-site Mg/Ca:
  16.35/3.25; Na-site Mg/Na: 14.06/1.87), but name
  `protstruct local metal geometry screen`, implemented in
  `scripts/checkmymetal_local.py`. It uses stored-coordinate contacts under
  3.20 Å, no symmetry expansion, and configured mean-distance dispersions.
  These dimensionless diagnostics are not validated significance tests,
  categorical exclusion of Mg, or positive identification of Ca/Na. The
  categorical ion conclusions, SNase biology and occupancy allowances are
  withdrawn; alternatives remain unresolved. No canonical service result or
  new local-screen execution is claimed.
- **RSCC alone does not validate ligand/water density (#694/#591).** The
  historical Ca/Na/sulfate RSCC values 0.9716/0.9636/0.9882 are informational.
  Matched limiting-radius metadata and independent RSZD/RSZO evidence are
  unavailable. Fixed RSCC cutoffs cannot establish that an ion is real or
  that water modelling is sound. The three water `density_misfit` labels are
  withdrawn; S680/S707/S729 retain historical RSCC 0.658/0.668/0.691 only as
  informational per-residue observations.
- **Similarity is not directional quality improvement (#695).** Historical
  lDDT 0.9725, Cα RMSD 0.423 Å and TM-score 0.987 suggest structural
  preservation relative to the tutorial input, not independent improvement.
  Their different methods and recorded residue coverage do not constitute
  one common alignment. The full original alignments and local arrays are
  unavailable; ten selected local values do not reproduce a 192-residue
  displacement distribution or a 96-residue lDDT distribution.
- **Difference-density peaks are not steric clashes (#747).** The old
  23-positive-plus-8-negative inventory is withdrawn from
  `T05_clashes_unique_pairs`. The genuine historical probe count of 11
  bad-overlap pairs is separate. Retained structured difference peaks keep
  historical magnitudes/locations with their original-output limitation;
  neither causal interpretations nor a round-7 omission claim survive.
- **Do not infer causes from unmatched historical numbers.** The correction
  retracts H-builder explanations for clashscore differences, restraint-library
  explanations for geometry offsets, and claims that NCS worsened round 7
  based on a mislabelled round-4 value. NCS's proposed effective data-to-parameter
  ratio remains an explicit unmeasured heuristic, not a reproduced statistic.
