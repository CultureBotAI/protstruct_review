### 6.2 Conditional Real-Algebra Representation Weighting vs. Exported `HKLOUT`
Let $k = \text{SCALE\_LS\_OVER}$, $E_i = \text{EXPAN}_i = \exp(-\text{SBS}_i)$, $b_0 = B_{\text{LS\_OVER}}$, and $C_i = \sum_m s_m \exp(-b_m q_i) F_{c,i,m}^{\text{pre}}$. In `SUMSFS_LS_DERIV`, $\text{YOS}_i = F_{o,i}^{\text{pre}} / k$ and $\text{YC\_ALL}_i = |C_i|\,E_i\exp(-b_0 q_i)$ (for `DBULK = 1`). Under **conditional real algebra** (assuming no intervening mutation of `FO`/`FC` after `APPLYSCALES` and equal $q_i$), `APPLYSCALES` + `MTZWRITE` export:
$$F_{\text{out},i} = \frac{F_{o,i}^{\text{pre}}}{k\,E_i} = \frac{\text{YOS}_i}{E_i}, \qquad \text{FC\_ALL\_LS}_{\text{out},i} = \left|C_i\exp(-b_0 q_i)\right| = \frac{\text{YC\_ALL}_i}{E_i}.$$
Because $E_i$ varies per reflection (`7,207` distinct captured binary32 `EXPAN` words across the `7,217` active rows in `replay_export.py -> distinct_captured_EXPAN_words`), $E_i^{-1}$ cancels in a single-row ratio but **does not cancel across a sum**:
$$R_{\text{native}} = \frac{\sum_i |\text{YOS}_i - \text{YC\_ALL}_i|}{\sum_i \text{YOS}_i} \neq \frac{\sum_i E_i^{-1}|\text{YOS}_i - \text{YC\_ALL}_i|}{\sum_i E_i^{-1}\text{YOS}_i} = R_{\text{export}}.$$
Earlier audit shorthand calling `FC_ALL_LS` an "unscaled" residual means only that the audit script applies no additional post-export scale factor; `F-obs` and `FC_ALL_LS` in `HKLOUT` are post-`APPLYSCALES` quantities in a different anisotropic representation weighting.
