## 2. Issue-Backed Review Corrections

### 2.1 Staged-Input and Keywords Final Relative Binding and Observation Retention ([#1307](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/protstruct_review/issues/1307))

- **Original failure**: In `scripts/onesar_refmac.py::_verify_immutable_baselines_post_run`, post-run verification of staged inputs and `refmac_keywords.txt` proved that the initially opened file descriptor remained regular and unchanged through EOF (`fstat`), but did not re-verify the relative leaf entry under the admitted parent directory descriptor after reading (or enforce final relative leaf size consistency against the captured read length), and outer exception handlers discarded completed-read digest/size/resolved-path observations when a later post-read check failed.
- **Corrected behavior**: Shared nested helper `_verify_admitted_leaf_post_run` inside `scripts/onesar_refmac.py::_verify_immutable_baselines_post_run` opens the parent directory via `_open_admitted_directory`, performs initial relative non-following `os.stat(leaf_name, dir_fd=parent_cap.fd, follow_symlinks=False)`, opens the relative leaf with `O_RDONLY | O_NONBLOCK | O_NOFOLLOW | O_CLOEXEC`, verifies pre-read and post-EOF `os.fstat` regular-file `(dev, ino)` identity and byte-length consistency (`initial_size`, `pre_read_size`, `post_fd_st.st_size`, and `len(raw_bytes)`), and—while both descriptors remain open—performs a final relative non-following `os.stat(leaf_name, dir_fd=parent_cap.fd, follow_symlinks=False)` requiring a non-symlink regular file whose `(dev, ino)` matches the opened descriptor (and baseline `(dev, ino)` when present) and whose `st_size` equals `captured_size_bytes`. It then re-verifies the ancestor chain (`_verify_admitted_ancestor_chain`) and admitted parent directory binding (`_reverify_admitted_directory_binding`). Once a size-consistent read completes (`read_completed = True`), `sha256`, `size_bytes`, and `resolved_path` (plus `actual_sha256` on the mutation entry) are retained across any subsequent `final_leaf_stat`, `post_read_ancestor_chain`, `post_read_parent_reverify`, or `descriptor_close` refusal while `matches_baseline` remains `False` and `verified` is `False`; incomplete or size-inconsistent reads do not populate completed-read fields. Both descriptors are closed in `finally`, attaching any secondary close `OSError` (`cleanup_close_error` / `close_error`) without masking an active primary exception.
- **Regression tests** (`scripts/test_onesar_refmac.py`):
  - `test_onesar_refmac.TestRefmacCollectorFirstSlice.test_immutable_postflight_retains_capture_when_leaf_replaced_during_read`
  - `test_onesar_refmac.TestRefmacCollectorFirstSlice.test_immutable_postflight_final_leaf_type_refusal`
  - `test_onesar_refmac.TestRefmacCollectorFirstSlice.test_immutable_postflight_rechecks_parent_and_ancestors`
  - `test_onesar_refmac.TestRefmacCollectorFirstSlice.test_collector_postflight_staged_leaf_replaced_during_first_read`
  - `test_onesar_refmac.TestRefmacCollectorFirstSlice.test_immutable_postflight_final_leaf_size_consistency`
- **Limits**: These checks establish discrete point-in-time observations at pre-read, EOF, and final relative/ancestor/parent recheck; they do not provide atomic namespace exclusion, continuous immutability, or detection of same-size in-place overwrites after EOF or transient swaps restored before the final check.

### 2.2 Collector Reader Nonblocking Admission and `fdopen` Descriptor Ownership ([#1308](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/protstruct_review/issues/1308))

- **Original failure**: In `scripts/onesar_refmac.py::_verify_and_read_admitted_input_file` and `_verify_immutable_baselines_post_run`, opening a path replaced by a writerless FIFO between pre-open `lstat`/`stat` and `os.open` could block indefinitely in `os.open` before `os.fstat` could reject the non-regular descriptor. In `_verify_and_read_admitted_input_file`, marking the descriptor owned before `os.fdopen(fd, "rb")` returned could also skip raw `os.close(fd)` if `os.fdopen` raised an ordinary construction error.
- **Corrected behavior**: Both `scripts/onesar_refmac.py::_verify_and_read_admitted_input_file` and `_verify_immutable_baselines_post_run` (`_verify_admitted_leaf_post_run`) include `getattr(os, "O_NONBLOCK", 0)` alongside `os.O_RDONLY | getattr(os, "O_NOFOLLOW", 0) | getattr(os, "O_CLOEXEC", 0)` and verify `stat.S_ISREG(fd_st.st_mode)` and `(dev, ino)` via `os.fstat` before reading. In `_verify_and_read_admitted_input_file`, `fd_owned_by_fileobj = True` is set only after `os.fdopen(fd, "rb")` succeeds, so raw `os.close(fd)` runs in the `except Exception` block if `os.fstat`, the inode check, or `os.fdopen` raises before file-object ownership is established.
- **Regression test** (`scripts/test_onesar_refmac.py`):
  - `test_onesar_refmac.TestRefmacCollectorFirstSlice.test_collector_readers_nonblocking_regular_file_admission` (exercises healthy regular-file controls and writerless-FIFO replacement refusals across admitted-input, staged-input, and keywords paths).
- **Limits**: Addresses POSIX nonblocking FIFO open admission and ordinary `Exception`-path descriptor ownership; it does not claim universal asynchronous interruption durability or dedicated fault injection of internal `os.fdopen` allocator failures.

### 2.3 Public Trace Reader Nonblocking Filesystem Admission and Snapshot Parity ([#1309](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/protstruct_review/issues/1309))

- **Original failure**: Direct filesystem calls to `scripts/refmac_diagnostic_trace.py::inspect_native_trace_envelope` opened `checked_path` without `O_NONBLOCK`, allowing a writerless FIFO substituted between `_check_non_symlink_regular_path` and `os.open` to block before `pre_read_fstat` regular-file validation.
- **Corrected behavior**: `scripts/refmac_diagnostic_trace.py::inspect_native_trace_envelope` opens filesystem trace paths with `os.O_RDONLY` plus `O_NONBLOCK`, `O_CLOEXEC`, and `O_NOFOLLOW` (when available on `os`), validates `stat.S_ISREG(fstat_pre.st_mode)` and `(st_dev, st_ino)` identity against `lstat_pre` at `stage = "pre_read_fstat"` (recording `code = "not_regular_file"` on FIFO replacement without reading), and closes `fd` in `finally`. In-memory snapshot routes (`inspect_native_trace_bytes`, `_preverified_trace_bytes_scope`, and explicit `raw_bytes`) stream retained bytes directly without reopening the filesystem path (`input_mode = "retained_bytes_snapshot"`, `filesystem_descriptor_opened = False`, `filesystem_identity_verified_by_trace_reader = False`), while failing closed (`code = "retained_trace_snapshot_unavailable"`) when `raw_bytes=None` is passed.
- **Regression tests** (`scripts/test_refmac_diagnostic_trace.py`):
  - `test_refmac_diagnostic_trace.TestRefmacDiagnosticTraceReader.test_filesystem_trace_reader_nonblocking_regular_file_admission_and_fifo_replacement`
  - `test_refmac_diagnostic_trace.TestRefmacDiagnosticTraceReader.test_retained_bytes_snapshot_mode_provenance_parity_and_missing_snapshot_contract`
- **Limits**: Retained-bytes snapshot parity covers parsed trace content (`valid`, `closed`, `event_count`, `footer_record_count`, digests, sizes, and `representation_header`), whereas provenance fields intentionally distinguish live filesystem verification from in-memory snapshot parsing. Nonblocking open admission is not a claim of whole-operation interruption durability.

### 2.4 Bounded Diagnostic Profile JSON Decode/Freeze Recursion Observation Retention ([#1168](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/protstruct_review/issues/1168))

- **Original failure**: In `scripts/refmac_diagnostic_profile.py`, a pinned outer manifest or inner role receipt (`source_manifest`, `source_audit`, `build_receipt`, `runtime_observation`) whose bytes matched its `FilePin` (`sha256`, `size_bytes`) could exceed Python's recursion limit during `json.loads` in `_parse_verified_json_bytes` or during recursive immutability freezing (`_deep_freeze_json_value`) in `_PinnedJsonDocument.__post_init__`, raising an uncaught `RecursionError` that bypassed `ProfileValidationError` and discarded the completed `FileObservation` and prior verified profile observations.
- **Corrected behavior**: `_parse_verified_json_bytes` catches `RecursionError` from `json.loads` and raises a `ValueError` chained (`from exc`) to the original `RecursionError`. `_read_pinned_json` constructs `_PinnedJsonDocument` inside its `try` block and catches both `ValueError` and `RecursionError` (from `_deep_freeze_json_value`), raising `_ObservedFilePinError(..., observation=observation, stage="manifest_json")` chained to the underlying exception. Outer manifest validation (`_validate_and_observe_outer_profile`) and inner role joins (`_read_and_verify_role_json` called from `_validate_source_manifest_join`, `_validate_source_audit_join`, `_validate_build_receipt_join`, and `_validate_runtime_observation_join` in `validate_profile`) translate those failures into `ProfileValidationError` with `failed_check = "manifest_json"` (outer) or `"<role>_join"` (inner), preserving `profile_files_verified = False`, `selected_executable = None`, the completed `FileObservation` (`read_complete = True`, `sha256`, `size_bytes`, `(dev, ino, mode, uid, gid)`, and dependencies), all previously verified file and root observations, and the original `RecursionError` in the cause chain (`error_records` / `cause_exception`). Process-control exceptions (`KeyboardInterrupt`, `SystemExit`) and unrelated `RuntimeError` subclasses are not caught by the recursion handler and propagate directly.
- **Regression tests** (`scripts/test_refmac_diagnostic_profile.py`):
  - `test_refmac_diagnostic_profile.TestTask990Issue1168PostEofObservations.test_1264_decoder_recursion_retains_outer_and_inner_json_observations`
  - `test_refmac_diagnostic_profile.TestTask990Issue1168PostEofObservations.test_1264_freezer_recursion_retains_outer_and_inner_json_observations`
  - `test_refmac_diagnostic_profile.TestTask990Issue1168PostEofObservations.test_1264_deep_pinned_outer_and_inner_json_fail_closed`
  - `test_refmac_diagnostic_profile.TestTask990Issue1168PostEofObservations.test_1264_json_boundary_process_control_exceptions_propagate`
- **Limits**: This slice is bounded to pinned JSON decode/freeze `RecursionError` observation retention in `scripts/refmac_diagnostic_profile.py`. It does not claim general resource-exhaustion or interruption durability, and issue [`#1168`](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/protstruct_review/issues/1168) remains open for its broader scope.

### 2.5 PHENIX Parent and Worker Primary-Failure Preservation ([#1310](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/protstruct_review/issues/1310))

- **Original failure**:
  - *Parent (`scripts/onesar_rerun.py`)*: In `run_phenix_rfactor_operation`, secondary `Exception`s raised while writing `phenix_build_admission_failure.json`, running post-failure staged-input SHA-256 postchecks, or inspecting/writing `phenix_operation_failure.json` in `_retain_parent_operation_failure` could replace the primary preflight, producer (`refinement_cache.OperationFailed`), or reconciliation (`ValueError`) exception, or misclassify a postcheck-only failure under the producer stage.
  - *Worker (`scripts/onesar_phenix_measure.py`)*: In `measure_first_block_rfactors`, secondary `Exception`s during `model_log.finalize()` or `fmodel_log.finalize()` in `finally` blocks, or during failure-checkpoint persistence (`_prepare_partial_evidence_for_checkpoint`, `_atomic_write_json_checkpoint`, `_checkpoint_diagnostics`) and `FAILURE DIAGNOSTIC` / `CHECKPOINT SERIALIZATION WARNING` `sys.stderr` writes/flushes, could replace the active primary measurement exception.
- **Corrected behavior**:
  - *Parent (`scripts/onesar_rerun.py`)*: `run_phenix_rfactor_operation` and `_retain_parent_operation_failure` attach secondary `Exception`s via `_build_phenix_secondary_failure_record` and `_attach_phenix_secondary_failure` to `exc.secondary_failures` in memory (and include `secondary_failures` / `staged_input_postchecks` in `phenix_build_admission_failure.json` or `phenix_operation_failure.json` when those checkpoint writes succeed) while re-raising the original primary exception (`ValueError`, `refinement_cache.OperationFailed`, or active `KeyboardInterrupt`/`SystemExit`). Staged-input postchecks run outside the `cached_phenix_operation` `try` block: if `cached_phenix_operation` succeeded and a postcheck fails, that postcheck `Exception` becomes the primary failure (`phenix_failed_stage = "staged_input_postcheck"`); if `cached_phenix_operation` already failed, postcheck errors attach to `producer_primary.secondary_failures`. If a new `KeyboardInterrupt` or `SystemExit` arises during secondary handling while a primary exception is active, `_chain_phenix_control_from_primary` attaches `primary_failure` and `secondary_failures` metadata in memory and raises `ctrl_exc from primary_exc`; `run_single_subject_rfactor_attempt` projects those attached fields into `error_record` (`parent_attempt_outcome.json::error`, persisted on disk when the exclusive parent outcome write succeeds, and attached in memory via `_attach_attempt_state_to_exception`).
  - *Worker (`scripts/onesar_phenix_measure.py`)*: `_finalize_log_stream_preserving_primary` finalizes `model_log` (`finalize_phase = "model_manager_log_finalize"`) and `fmodel_log` (`finalize_phase = "fmodel_update_all_scales_log_finalize"`) once: when `active_primary` is `None`, a finalizer `Exception` raises as the primary failure; when `active_primary` is present, a secondary finalizer `Exception` is attached in memory via `_build_worker_secondary_failure_record` and `_attach_worker_secondary_failure` to `active_primary.secondary_failures` (and `secondary_failures`) while `active_primary` continues to propagate. For ordinary `Exception` primaries, the outer `except Exception as exc` block attempts to persist `failure_record` and `secondary_failures` to `phenix_rfactors_partial_evidence.json` and `phenix_diagnostics.json` (which record them on disk when those checkpoint writes succeed); active `KeyboardInterrupt` and `SystemExit` primaries bypass the outer `except Exception` failure-checkpoint path and retain their attached `secondary_failures` metadata in memory. Within `except Exception as exc`, secondary failures at `failure_partial_evidence_checkpoint`, `failure_diagnostics_checkpoint`, `failure_diagnostic_stderr`, or `checkpoint_serialization_warning_stderr` are attached to `exc.secondary_failures` and the in-memory `failure_record` / `diagnostics` / `partial_evidence` structures without replacing `exc`, and late `stderr` metadata is not retroactively written into JSON checkpoints persisted earlier. Any newly raised `KeyboardInterrupt` or `SystemExit` during secondary finalization/reporting remains chained via `_chain_worker_control_from_primary` (`raise ctrl_exc from active_primary` / `raise ctrl_exc from exc`).
- **Regression tests** (`scripts/test_onesar_rerun.py`):
  - `test_onesar_rerun.TestTask179CanaryCliAndAttemptOrchestration.test_phenix_parent_primary_failure_preservation_1310`
  - `test_onesar_rerun.TestPhenixReconciliationAndPureHelpers.test_helper_pure_functions_and_python39_static_contract`
  - `test_onesar_rerun.TestPhenixReconciliationAndPureHelpers.test_in_process_measure_first_block_rfactors_with_source_backed_doubles_1017`
- **Limits**: Covers primary-failure preservation across ordinary secondary retention, checkpoint, and `stderr` failures plus explicit cause-chaining when a new `KeyboardInterrupt`/`SystemExit` occurs during secondary handling, with on-disk failure/outcome persistence conditioned on those writes succeeding. It does not claim universal process-interruption durability, and broader [`#1014`](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/protstruct_review/issues/1014) remains open.
