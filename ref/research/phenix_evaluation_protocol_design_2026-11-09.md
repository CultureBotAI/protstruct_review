## 1. Three Separated Evaluation Questions and Four Historical Design Lessons

Every benchmark evaluation must keep three questions separate (`ref/quality_reporting.md:166–195`, `ref/research/refinement_assessment_case_study_2026-10-18.md:21–35`):

1. **`implementation_agreement`:** What agreement or divergence between independent software implementations is observed on the **exact same** coordinate and reflection inputs, and under what documented selection, scaling, dictionary, or protonation conventions?
2. **`final_model_quality_support`:** What measurements support or limit the final model's suitability for its intended use in its resolution (`d_min`), data-quality (`T13`), and local-site context?
3. **`change_relative_to_input`:** What improved, remained invariant, or regressed between the **actual starting model/data** and the refined output on matched reflections and matched atom selections (`ref/quality_reporting.md:166–178`)?

Retained repository audits establish four concise design constraints for `#1048` (cited to committed sources without repeating retrospective numerical tables):

- **Lesson 1 — Exact subject, archive-member, and selection provenance (`#1226`, `#1235`):** Evaluation must bind to retained coordinate and reflection subjects (`ref/research/data/refinement_assessment_1sar_and_controls_2026-10-18.json` and members inside `data/coscientists/openscientist/cdba2c07-daff-4f60-ae96-12452b3a5fbb_artifacts.zip`) and cannot substitute retained trajectory-log or report claims for measurements on absent coordinates (in `1SAR`, the packaged Round-4 coordinate member is retained, whereas the Round-7 trajectory log and final report are retained in the archive while the Round-7 coordinate subject is unarchived, preventing independent Round-7 coordinate adjudication; archive member names are not standalone files on `main`), and must record whenever historical evaluator outputs lack retained effective reflection denominators or selections (`ref/research/refinement_assessment_case_study_2026-10-18.md:44–52, 59–72, 107–117`).
- **Lesson 2 — Agreement, endpoint quality, and directional change do not collapse into a single verdict (`#1227`):** Multi-tool agreement on a static model does not prove correctness (`ref/quality_reporting.md:193–194`), solver spread without single-factor isolation does not diagnose a single cause (`ref/research/refinement_assessment_case_study_2026-10-18.md:25–26, 67–68`), and historical two-family conjunction rules in `ref/research/negative_control_round3.md:16–29` (`ref/research/data/negative_control_round3_bench.json`) are diagnostic constructs rather than a substitute for reporting separated fit, geometry, and displacement changes.
- **Lesson 3 — Evaluator ADP/input sensitivity is distinct from refinement-profile efficacy (`#1229`, `#1230`):** Post-hoc evaluator settings—such as `REFMAC5` `BREF ISOT` vs. `BREF ANIS` on the regenerated-null shared-sign population (`ref/research/negative_control_round9.md:38–48`, `ref/research/data/negative_control_round9_anis.json`)—and producer-specific input hazards—such as `HLA/HLB/HLC/HLD` phase columns triggering `MLHL` target selection in `PHENIX` refinement and its phase-free null re-screen (`ref/research/negative_control_round5.md:54–60`, `ref/research/negative_control_round6.md:40–44`) vs. `REFMAC5` ligand/record reader constraints (`MAKE NEWLIGAND CONTINUE` and `CSO`/`CYS` `ATOM`/`ANISOU` `label_comp_id` mismatch in `ref/research/negative_control_round6.md:22–28`, `ref/research/data/negative_control_round6_hygiene.json`)—affect measurement comparability, not the efficacy of a refinement profile; unverified causal claims about `2VXN` or default hydrogen behavior are excluded.
- **Lesson 4 — Multi-round historical series are confounded and retain named exceptions (`#1228`, `#1231`, `#1232`):** Across the sub-angstrom control series (`ref/research/data/negative_control_round2_enrolled.json`, where inherited registration `stratum` flags differ from physical `d_min` values; plain recovery without `ordered_solvent` in `ref/research/negative_control_round4.md:27–45` and `ref/research/data/negative_control_round4_recover.json`; `ordered_solvent` in `ref/research/negative_control_round5.md` and `ref/research/data/negative_control_round5_recover.json`; and `phenix.ready_set` riding hydrogens plus `ordered_solvent` in `ref/research/negative_control_round10.md:42–49, 91–94` and `ref/research/data/negative_control_round10_recover.json`), perturbations, protonation, solvent building, and evaluator settings changed across rounds. Those historical rounds are multi-factor protocol contrasts rather than single-variable ablations, and nominal recovery counts retain the named `W4` cross-oracle contradiction (`2VXN`, `w4_contradictions: 1`) as an explicit scientific exception (`ref/research/negative_control_round10.md:42–49, 91–94`).

## 2. Required Benchmark Arm Families and Resource/Scope Contract (`C1`, `C6`, `C8`, `V4`, `V5`)

A comparative evaluation of agentic PHENIX refinement requires **three distinct arm families**:

1. **Arm Family 1 — Agentic PHENIX controller arm(s):**
   - An autonomous or LLM/tool-calling controller driving PHENIX refinement (either an external co-scientist workflow or PHENIX's bundled `libtbx.langchain` agent).
   - **Bundled planner provenance (`V5`, `#1222`):** In `PHENIX_PREFIX/lib/python3.9/site-packages/libtbx/langchain/agent/planner.py:1111–1125, 1340–1362` and `libtbx/langchain/validation/mtz_utils.py:86–106`, `generate_next_move` invokes the LLM chain first and constructs the mechanical command (`construct_command_mechanically`) immediately afterward as a separately returned backup field rather than invoking it only inside an exception handler; `add_rfree_generation_if_needed` inspects the first whitespace `.mtz` token not starting with `-` (returning the command unchanged when no such token is present). Agent arms must log prompts, responses, selected vs. backup commands, and effective `.eff` files.
2. **Arm Family 2 — Qualified adaptive human refiner arm(s):**
   - Practicing macromolecular crystallographer(s) refining the identical frozen starting inputs under pre-registered expertise criteria, participant/repeat design, allowed interactive tools, and stopping budgets.
   - **GUI settings are not a human arm (`C1`, `V4`, `#1221`):** Running `phenix.refine` with parameters initialized by the `wxGUI2` interface is a fixed software baseline variant, **not** an adaptive human refiner arm. In `PHENIX_PREFIX/lib/python3.9/site-packages/wxGUI2/Programs/Refine/Main.py:17, 53–166`, `Config.py:307`, and `Dialogs.py:30–76`, `setup_app_local` sets `r_free_flags.generate=False` (`:58`), calls `process_default_settings` (`:61–70`, defined at `:81–166`) to merge user/project saved defaults only when `not self._restore_mode`, and only afterward sets `ordered_solvent.mode="second_half"` when still `None` (`:71–74`), while GUI dialogs expose manual atom-selection controls rather than enforced CLI equivalence.
3. **Arm Family 3 — Fixed PHENIX baseline arm(s) (never pooled across sub-variants):**
   - **3a — Out-of-box default fixed PHENIX:** Unmodified `phenix.refine` with static PHIL defaults (`PHENIX_PREFIX/lib/python3.9/site-packages/phenix/refinement/__init__.params:111–119, 327, 355, 477`).
   - **3b — Resolution-conditioned / configured fixed PHENIX (paired with `#1047`):** Fixed non-adaptive PHIL configurations chosen under [`phenix_resolution_profile_decisions_2026-11-09.md`](phenix_resolution_profile_decisions_2026-11-09.md). Variants `3a` and `3b` must be reported as separate arms (or four total arms) and **never pooled**.

**Unselected arm-scope and resource contract parameters (require user/scientific selection):**
- **Program scope:** `phenix.refine` only vs. multi-program PHENIX workflows (`phenix.ready_set`, `phenix.ligandfit`, `phenix.autobuild`, `phenix.molprobity`).
- **Human refiner protocol:** Qualification requirements, number of human participants, within- and between-refiner repeats, and permitted interactive rebuilding software (e.g., `Coot` / `ISOLDE` vs. PHENIX-only).
- **Information and tool parity:** Whether controllers and humans receive identical sequence files, ligand chemical descriptors (SMILES/CIF), map inspection tools, and validation feedback.
- **Resource and stopping budgets:** Wall-clock time limits, CPU/GPU allocation, maximum refinement macro-cycles / program invocations, LLM token/tool-call caps, and stopping criteria.
- **Factorial vs. one-factor sensitivity design (`C8`):** If individual PHIL mechanisms (`ready_set`, `ordered_solvent`, ADP mode, weight search) are varied, the protocol must either specify a crossed factorial matrix (to estimate main and interaction effects) or label one-factor-at-a-time arms strictly as single-factor sensitivity comparisons without interaction claims.

## 3. Matched Input Provenance, Integrity Preprocessing, and Failure Retention (`C3`, `C6`, `C7`)

Every arm evaluated on target $i$ must share a frozen, hash-pinned input bundle:

1. **Matched actual starting coordinates and reflections:**
   - All arms start from the **exact same starting coordinate bytes** (never assuming the starting model equals the deposited PDB structure; `ref/quality_reporting.md:199–200`) and **exact same reflection file bytes**, with pre-declared amplitude/sigma/flag column labels, free-flag test value, unit cell, space group, indexing, and full input-defined HKL domain (`ref/thresholds_and_standards.md:171–202`).
2. **Common integrity preprocessing vs. arm-specific interventions (`C6`):**
   - Disclosed input-integrity hygiene (such as stripping phase columns `HLA/HLB/HLC/HLD` so `PHENIX` does not switch to `MLHL`, or auditing MTZ header formatting) must be applied identically before branching to any arm.
   - By contrast, model-modifying or refinement-policy steps (`phenix.ready_set` hydrogen addition, `ordered_solvent=True`, or custom ADP selections) are **arm-specific interventions**; applying them to the out-of-box default arm (`3a`) would invalidate it as an unconfigured baseline.
3. **Merged amplitudes vs. intensities and `T03` retention scope:**
   - `scripts/t03_reflection_retention.py` currently audits little-endian, unique-HKL merged-amplitude MTZ files (`ref/thresholds_and_standards.md:194–199`). Whether intensity-only datasets are excluded or converted via a pre-registered `French–Wilson` / `ctruncate` step before trial intake is an explicit design choice.
4. **MTZ free-flag availability vs. deposited $R_{\text{free}}$ header scalar (`C7`):**
   - Having a valid, frozen work/free flag partition in the input MTZ (required for `T03` reflection retention and paired $\Delta R_{\text{free}}$ evaluation) is distinct from having a non-null deposited $R_{\text{free}}$ scalar in the historical PDB header (`ref/thresholds_and_standards.md:163–169`). A missing deposited $R_{\text{free}}$ scalar renders only the deposition-comparison clause unevaluable (`deposition_clause: "unevaluable"`); whether to restrict target enrollment to entries with a deposited $R_{\text{free}}$ scalar is a separate optional cohort choice.
5. **Conventions and failed/incommensurable arm retention (`C3`):**
   - Record restraint libraries/CIFs, alternate-conformer handling, hydrogen convention (`electron` vs. `nuclear`), ADP mode, solvent policy, and effective configuration for every run.
   - Any arm that crashes, drops reflections, alters free-set flags, or produces an unparsable model must be **retained in the full scheduled trial denominator** with an explicit failure/incommensurability classification; intersection-only reporting over successful runs is prohibited.

## 4. Candidate Primary Estimand and Paired Between-Arm Contrasts (`C3`)

On target $i$, for arm $a$ and reciprocal-space evaluator $e$ evaluated on the identical reflection domain and frozen free-flag assignment (`ref/quality_reporting.md:166–169`):

$$\Delta_{a,i}^{(e)} = R_{\text{free}}^{(e)}(\text{final}_{a,i}) - R_{\text{free}}^{(e)}(\text{actual\_start}_i)$$

The candidate primary paired between-arm contrast between arm $a$ (e.g., `agent`) and comparator arm $b$ (e.g., `human`, `fixed_default`, or `fixed_configured`) on target $i$ is:

$$\delta_{a,b,i}^{(e)} = \Delta_{a,i}^{(e)} - \Delta_{b,i}^{(e)}$$

- **Directional interpretation boundary (`ref/quality_reporting.md:166–169`):** A negative $\Delta_{a,i}^{(e)}$ or $\delta_{a,b,i}^{(e)}$ indicates lower cross-validation error on evaluator $e$ only; it does not by itself establish statistical significance, clinical/biological equivalence, or net multi-family benefit. Absolute $R_{\text{free}}$, $R_{\text{work}}$, $R_{\text{free}} - R_{\text{work}}$ (`T03_r-free_r-work_gap`), stereochemistry (`T05`), real-space density fit (`T06`, `T10`), coordinate movement/accuracy (`T01`), and interface quality (`T16`) must be co-reported alongside $\delta_{a,b,i}^{(e)}$.
- **Unselected statistical design parameters (no fabricated $N$ or margins):** The user/scientific lead must select: (i) the primary contrast(s) (`agent − human`, `agent − fixed_default`, `agent − fixed_configured`), (ii) the primary evaluator $e$ or multi-oracle adjudication rule, (iii) the unit of analysis, clustering (by target, resolution tier, human participant, or repeat seed), and summary location estimator, (iv) uncertainty/confidence-interval construction, target sample size $N$, and any non-inferiority/equivalence margin, (v) multiplicity adjustment across arms/strata, and (vi) pre-registered handling of failed or incommensurable runs.

## 5. Holdout Partition, Blinding, and $R_{\text{free}}$ Feedback Governance (`C2`)

1. **Development vs. held-out target inventory:**
   - Partition targets into a **development/calibration set** (used to tune prompts, controller heuristics, or resolution-profile hypotheses) and a **frozen held-out evaluation set** deduplicated by sequence identity and fold/homolog family. The existing 22-entry sub-angstrom control set and `1SAR` case study belong to development/calibration history and cannot be treated as an unseen holdout cohort.
2. **Participant vs. assessor blinding and contamination limits:**
   - Separate **execution blinding** (what human refiners and agent controllers can see regarding PDB IDs, deposited coordinates, homolog structures, or comparator arms) from **assessor/adjudicator blinding** (evaluating anonymized output models without arm labels).
   - Explicitly record whether sequence, ligand identity, or header metadata are provided. Because public crystal structures and sequences may exist in LLM pre-training corpora and unit-cell/sequence fingerprints allow target identification, blinding rules mitigate direct file access but cannot guarantee zero pre-training contamination.
3. **Governance of $R_{\text{free}}$ during refinement vs. outer-loop selection:**
   - Freeze the MTZ free-flag mask before trial start and verify exact flag retention post-run (`ref/thresholds_and_standards.md:183–192`).
   - Distinguish **standard internal algorithmic use** of the free set inside a refinement program (e.g., maximum-likelihood $\sigma_A$ and internal weight scaling) from **discretionary outer-loop selection leakage** (running multiple trajectories, parameter sweeps, or agent iterations and selecting the winner or stopping point by repeatedly inspecting $R_{\text{free}}$).
   - Whether to (a) restrict outer-loop controller/human model selection to work-set, geometry, and map-likelihood criteria while keeping $R_{\text{free}}$ masked until final evaluation, or (b) partition a **sealed secondary holdout reflection set** (`R_sleep` / second test set) is an explicit scientific decision—never performed by silently regenerating flags or altering the baseline arm's reflection set.
   - Each arm must pre-register a deterministic **final candidate-selection rule** before outcomes are inspected.

## 7. Metric-Specific Independence, Comparability, and Empty Criterion Bindings (`N11`, `#1236`)

1. **Metric-specific independence (`ref/quality_reporting.md:182–195`):** Independence is determined per observable by the executing binary, restraint library, and atom/reflection selector—never by wrapper names (`MolProbity` shares `cctbx` components with `PHENIX`).
2. **Coordinate movement vs. accuracy (`ref/quality_reporting.md:171–175`):** `T01_ca_rmsd_å` (start, final) measures movement only; accuracy requires evaluating both `start` and `final` against the same external reference under an identical residue/atom bijection.
3. **Geometry libraries and populations (`ref/structural_criteria.yaml:485–505`):** `phenix.model_statistics` `covalent geometry : bond` scores the covalent bond population against the Conformation-Dependent Library (`CDL`), distinct from its headline `Bond:` line (which adds disulfide/link restraints), whereas `gemmi rmsz` scores against the static CCP4 monomer library (`$CLIBD_MON`). (`gemmi validate` is catalogued separately under `T06` in `ref/catalog.yaml:726–729` for file/dictionary checks, not coordinate RMSZ.)
4. **Hydrogen, clashscore, Ramachandran, rotamer, and flip calibration limits (`ref/threshold_evidence_status.yaml:78–97, 124–150, 567–575`, `ref/catalog.yaml:413–427, 1460–1478`):**
   - **Clashscore (`#798`, `#799`, `#790`):** `changed_pipeline` is `policy_state: not_evaluable` (`evidence_state: unmeasured`); matching `reduce`/`probe` version pins (`PHENIX 2.0-5936`, Richardson `reduce 4.16.250520`, standalone `probe 2.26.021123`) is necessary but not sufficient when hydrogen-building flags or ligand dictionaries change.
   - **Ramachandran and rotamers (`#786`, `#766`):** `shared_classification` (`>= 0.99`) is a documentary floor only for `PHENIX 2.0-5936` vs. the same-subject wwPDB validation report on insertion-code-keyed shared residues; `raw_rama_percent` is `informational`, and `rotamer_favored` is `policy_state: policy_unresolved` (`evidence_state: unmeasured`).
   - **N/Q/H flip conflicts (`#809`, `#1236`):** `flip_conflict` (`T14_asn_gln_his_flip_set_conflicts`) is `policy_state: suspended` (`evidence_state: producer_mismatch`) because the retained round 47/48 comparison actually benchmarked **PHENIX-distributed `reduce` against `mmtbx.reduce2`** (both `cctbx`-family) rather than an independent standalone `reduce` pair (`ref/threshold_evidence_status.yaml:567–575`).
5. **Density, unmerged data, and interface limits:**
   - `T06_residue_rscc`, `T10_ligand_rscc`, and `T10_ligand_rsr` are conditioned on map type and atomic limiting-radius convention and carry no universal B-factor-independent quality cutoff (`ref/quality_reporting.md:215, 229–230`).
