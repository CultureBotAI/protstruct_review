## 1. Three Separated Evaluation Questions and Four Historical Design Lessons

Every benchmark evaluation must keep three questions separate (`ref/quality_reporting.md:166–195`, `ref/research/refinement_assessment_case_study_2026-10-18.md:21–35`):

1. **`implementation_agreement`:** What agreement or divergence between independent software implementations is observed on the **exact same** coordinate and reflection inputs, and under what documented selection, scaling, dictionary, or protonation conventions?
2. **`final_model_quality_support`:** What measurements support or limit the final model's suitability for its intended use in its resolution (`d_min`), data-quality (`T13`), and local-site context?
3. **`change_relative_to_input`:** What improved, remained invariant, or regressed between the **actual starting model/data** and the refined output on matched reflections and matched atom selections (`ref/quality_reporting.md:166–178`)?

Retained repository audits establish four concise design constraints for `#1048` (cited to committed sources without repeating retrospective numerical tables):

- **Lesson 1 — Exact subject, archive-member, and selection provenance (`#1226`, `#1235`):** Evaluation must bind to retained coordinate and reflection subjects (`ref/research/data/refinement_assessment_1sar_and_controls_2026-10-18.json` and members inside `data/coscientists/openscientist/cdba2c07-daff-4f60-ae96-12452b3a5fbb_artifacts.zip`) and cannot substitute retained trajectory-log or report claims for measurements on absent coordinates (in `1SAR`, the packaged Round-4 coordinate member is retained, whereas the Round-7 trajectory log and final report are retained in the archive while the Round-7 coordinate subject is unarchived, preventing independent Round-7 coordinate adjudication; archive member names are not standalone files on `main`), and must record whenever historical evaluator outputs lack retained effective reflection denominators or selections (`ref/research/refinement_assessment_case_study_2026-10-18.md:44–52, 59–72, 107–117`).
- **Lesson 2 — Agreement, endpoint quality, and directional change do not collapse into a single verdict (`#1227`):** Multi-tool agreement on a static model does not prove correctness (`ref/quality_reporting.md:193–194`), solver spread without single-factor isolation does not diagnose a single cause (`ref/research/refinement_assessment_case_study_2026-10-18.md:25–26, 67–68`), and historical two-family conjunction rules in `ref/research/negative_control_round3.md:16–29` (`ref/research/data/negative_control_round3_bench.json`) are diagnostic constructs rather than a substitute for reporting separated fit, geometry, and displacement changes.
- **Lesson 3 — Evaluator ADP/input sensitivity is distinct from refinement-profile efficacy (`#1229`, `#1230`):** Post-hoc evaluator settings—such as `REFMAC5` `BREF ISOT` vs. `BREF ANIS` on the regenerated-null shared-sign population (`ref/research/negative_control_round9.md:38–48`, `ref/research/data/negative_control_round9_anis.json`)—and producer-specific input hazards—such as `HLA/HLB/HLC/HLD` phase columns triggering `MLHL` target selection in `PHENIX` refinement and its phase-free null re-screen (`ref/research/negative_control_round5.md:54–60`, `ref/research/negative_control_round6.md:40–44`) vs. `REFMAC5` ligand/record reader constraints (`MAKE NEWLIGAND CONTINUE` and `CSO`/`CYS` `ATOM`/`ANISOU` `label_comp_id` mismatch in `ref/research/negative_control_round6.md:22–28`, `ref/research/data/negative_control_round6_hygiene.json`)—affect measurement comparability, not the efficacy of a refinement profile; unverified causal claims about `2VXN` or default hydrogen behavior are excluded.
- **Lesson 4 — Multi-round historical series are confounded and retain named exceptions (`#1231`, `#1232`, `#1228`):** Across the sub-angstrom control series (`ref/research/data/negative_control_round2_enrolled.json`, where inherited registration `stratum` flags differ from physical `d_min` values; plain recovery without `ordered_solvent` in `ref/research/negative_control_round4.md:27–45` and `ref/research/data/negative_control_round4_recover.json`; `ordered_solvent` in `ref/research/negative_control_round5.md` and `ref/research/data/negative_control_round5_recover.json`; and `phenix.ready_set` riding hydrogens plus `ordered_solvent` in `ref/research/negative_control_round10.md:42–49, 91–94` and `ref/research/data/negative_control_round10_recover.json`), perturbations, protonation, solvent building, and evaluator settings changed across rounds. Those historical rounds are multi-factor protocol contrasts rather than single-variable ablations, and nominal recovery counts retain the named `W4` cross-oracle contradiction (`2VXN`, `w4_contradictions: 1`) as an explicit scientific exception (`ref/research/negative_control_round10.md:42–49, 91–94`).

## 3. Matched Input Provenance, Held-Out Intake Selection, and Failure Retention (`D1`, `D3`, `D4b`, `P5b`, `C3`, `C6`, `C7`)

Every arm evaluated on target $i$ shares frozen, hash-pinned input provenance under the following selected intake and retention rules:

1. **Three distinguished artifact roles — immutable parent bundle, common hash-pinned analysis derivative, and unmodified `Arm 3a` reference (`D4b`, `P5b`, `#1239`):**
   - **(a) Immutable parent input bundle:** The original enrolled coordinate and reflection file bytes, never mutated in place.
   - **(b) Common hash-pinned analysis derivative:** The shared coordinate and MTZ bundle produced after common pre-branch integrity hygiene (such as stripping `HLA/HLB/HLC/HLD` phase columns so `PHENIX` does not switch to `MLHL`) and after partitioning/sealing the secondary reflection holdout set `R_sleep` alongside the frozen standard `R_free` set. All comparable arms start from the **exact same starting coordinate bytes** (`ref/quality_reporting.md:199–200`) and **exact same common derivative MTZ bytes** (`ref/thresholds_and_standards.md:171–202`). Derived MTZ bytes are never claimed to equal the parent MTZ bytes, and standard `R_free` flags are never silently regenerated.
   - **(c) Unmodified-MTZ `Arm 3a` reference run and isolated `R_sleep`-withholding control (`#1239`):** Retain the user-selected original/unmodified-MTZ `Arm 3a` reference run on the parent MTZ to test for possible internal maximum-likelihood $\sigma_A$ or weight perturbation. Because common input hygiene (e.g., stripping `HLA/HLB/HLC/HLD` phase columns, which can switch `PHENIX` from `ML` to `MLHL`; `ref/research/negative_control_round5.md:54–60`, `ref/research/negative_control_round6.md:40–44`) also differs when a parent MTZ requires hygiene edits, an isolated attribution of `R_sleep` withholding vs. pre-withholding scaling requires either an additional **hygiene-matched pre-`R_sleep` control** or restricting that isolated withholding attribution to parent MTZs already identical to the hygienic input. This is a **future required verification check, not an available scale-invariance conclusion**, and concrete control construction and target eligibility remain in preregistration.
2. **Common integrity preprocessing vs. arm-specific interventions (`C6`, `D2`/`P2`):**
   - Disclosed input-integrity hygiene applies identically before branching to any arm.
   - Model-modifying or refinement-policy steps (`phenix.ready_set` in `Arm 3b-plus-readyset`, `ordered_solvent=True`, or custom ADP selections) are **arm-specific interventions**; `Arm 3a` runs with literal **zero PHIL overrides**, and any target where default column/flag autodetection fails to match the frozen observation labels or free-flag test value is retained as failed/incommensurable rather than quietly adding CLI overrides to `Arm 3a`.
3. **Selected held-out cohort exclusions and merged-amplitude MTZ intake (`D1`):**
   - The held-out evaluation cohort is a **sequence- and fold/homolog-deduplicated target set excluding all 22 historical sub-angstrom control structures and `1SAR`**.
   - Target intake is restricted to **merged-amplitude MTZ files** (`F/SIGF`, compatible with `scripts/t03_reflection_retention.py`; `ref/thresholds_and_standards.md:194–199`) carrying a valid, frozen work/free flag partition.
4. **Selected deposited $R_{\text{free}}$ header scalar policy (`D1`, `C7`):**
   - Having a valid, frozen work/free flag partition in the input MTZ is distinct from having a non-null deposited $R_{\text{free}}$ scalar in the historical PDB header (`ref/thresholds_and_standards.md:163–169`). Under the user's `2026-11-10` selection, **missing a deposited header $R_{\text{free}}$ scalar does not exclude a target with a valid frozen MTZ free mask**: for such targets, the deposition comparison is recorded as `deposition_clause: "unevaluable"` while paired between-arm evaluation proceeds on the frozen MTZ flags. Concrete target inventory, per-tier enrollment counts, sample size $N$, and margins remain the next preregistration step.
5. **Conventions and full-denominator dual failure accounting (`D3`, `C3`):**
   - Record restraint libraries/CIFs, alternate-conformer handling, hydrogen convention (`electron` vs. `nuclear`), ADP mode, solvent policy, and effective `.eff` configuration for every run.
   - Every arm that crashes, drops reflections, alters free-set flags, fails default autodetection in `Arm 3a`, or produces an unparsable/incommensurable model remains in the **full scheduled trial denominator** and is reported using **both (i) worst-rank imputation (for rank-based summaries, never treating ordinal ranks as numerical $\Delta R_{\text{free}}$ deltas) and (ii) explicit per-arm and per-stratum completion rates**. Intersection-only reporting over successful runs is prohibited.

## 4. Selected Co-Primary Estimands, Evaluators, and Statistical Preregistration Boundary (`D3`, `C3`)

On target $i$, for arm $a$ and reciprocal-space evaluator $e$ evaluated on the identical reflection domain and frozen free-flag assignment (`ref/quality_reporting.md:166–169`):

$$\Delta_{a,i}^{(e)} = R_{\text{free}}^{(e)}(\text{final}_{a,i}) - R_{\text{free}}^{(e)}(\text{actual\_start}_i)$$

The paired between-arm contrast between arm $a$ and comparator arm $b$ on target $i$ is:

$$\delta_{a,b,i}^{(e)} = \Delta_{a,i}^{(e)} - \Delta_{b,i}^{(e)}$$

- **Selected co-primary contrasts and active evaluators (`D3a`, `D3b`, `#1048` `C3`):**
  - Paired contrasts $\delta_{a,b,i}^{(e)}$ on $\Delta R_{\text{free}}$ (`agent − human` and `agent − fixed` across the unpooled variants `1a`, `1b`, `2`, `3a`, `3b-refine-only`, `3b-plus-readyset`) are selected as **co-primary**.
  - Future evaluations will **co-report `phenix.model_vs_data` (`T03`/`T06`) and `gemmi sfcalc`** as the selected reciprocal-space evaluators (without averaging values across tools), while **`REFMAC5` remains gated on `#1102` and `#1109`–`#1112`** (Section 9). The user selected the tool pairing, not the concrete `gemmi` $R_{\text{free}}$ estimator (`scripts/gemmi_rfactor.py:10–34` distinguishes globally scaled direct-sum `FC` from additional work-fitted bin-wise rescaling, which have different retained comparison scopes); exact mask, scaling/rescaling, reflection-denominator, and holdout conventions remain preregistration work.
  - The full canonical endpoint inventory in Section 6 (`T03`, `T05`, `T06`, `T10`, `T01`, `T02`, `T13`, conditional `T16`) is preserved and co-reported alongside $\delta_{a,b,i}^{(e)}$.
- **Directional interpretation boundary (`ref/quality_reporting.md:166–169`):** A negative $\Delta_{a,i}^{(e)}$ or $\delta_{a,b,i}^{(e)}$ indicates lower cross-validation error on evaluator $e$ only; it does not by itself establish statistical significance, biological equivalence, or net multi-family benefit.
- **Selected dual failure reporting (`D3c`) vs. unresolved preregistration estimators (no fabricated $N$ or margins):** Failed/incommensurable runs remain in the full scheduled denominator with **both worst-rank imputation and per-stratum completion rates**. Exact `gemmi` mask/scaling/denominator/holdout conventions (`scripts/gemmi_rfactor.py:10–34`), the contrast family hierarchy across the five computational variants and staged human arm, rank/tie/missingness rules for worst-rank imputation (never treating ranks as numerical $R_{\text{free}}$ deltas), summary location estimator, clustering (by target, resolution tier, human participant, or repeat seed), uncertainty/confidence-interval construction, multiplicity control, sample size $N$, and non-inferiority/equivalence margins remain to be fixed in the result-free preregistration commit.

## 5. Selected Holdout Freeze, Blinding, and Dual $R_{\text{free}}$ / $R_{\text{sleep}}$ Governance (`D4a`, `D4b`, `P5a`, `P5b`, `C2`, `#1239`)

1. **Selected development freeze before held-out unlock (`P5a`, `D1`):**
   - All prompt engineering, controller heuristic tuning, and candidate resolution-profile selection (`Arm 3b-refine-only` and `Arm 3b-plus-readyset`) are **frozen on the existing development/calibration set (`22` sub-angstrom control structures + `1SAR`)** before unlocking the sequence- and fold/homolog-deduplicated held-out evaluation cohort (which excludes all 22 controls and `1SAR`).
2. **Selected execution masking, assessor anonymization, and contamination limits (`D4a`):**
   - **Execution blinding:** Mask PDB IDs and identifying header records during controller (`1a`, `1b`) and human (`2`) execution while preserving required crystallographic metadata (unit cell, space group, resolution/wavelength, and track-specified sequence/ligand inputs).
   - **Assessor/adjudicator blinding:** Anonymize output models and strip arm labels before evaluation and active-site adjudication.
   - **Explicit contamination boundary:** Header and PDB-ID masking prevents direct identifier lookup, but because public crystal structures and sequences may exist in LLM pre-training corpora and unit-cell/sequence fingerprints permit target identification, anonymity **does not guarantee zero pre-training knowledge**; this limitation must be recorded in every trial report.
3. **Selected dual $R_{\text{free}}$ governance and sealed secondary holdout `R_sleep` (`D4b`, `P5b`, `#1239`):**
   - The user selected **both** outer-loop selection governance and a sealed secondary reflection holdout set alongside the frozen standard `R_free` partition:
     1. **No discretionary outer-loop $R_{\text{free}}$ selection:** Pre-declared deterministic candidate-selection and stopping rules based on work-set fit, stereochemical geometry, and map/LLG criteria govern candidate selection across trajectories/iterations, forbidding outer-loop cherry-picking on standard `R_free`.
     2. **Sealed secondary reflection holdout (`R_sleep`) and reference controls (`#1239`):** Partition and seal a secondary holdout reflection subset (`R_sleep`) alongside the frozen standard `R_free` set in the common hash-pinned analysis derivative shared by comparable arms, and retain the user-selected **unmodified-MTZ `Arm 3a` reference run** on the parent MTZ to test for possible internal maximum-likelihood $\sigma_A$ perturbation. Isolating `R_sleep` withholding from common input hygiene (such as stripping `HLA/HLB/HLC/HLD` phase columns that can switch `PHENIX` from `ML` to `MLHL`; `ref/research/negative_control_round5.md:54–60`, `ref/research/negative_control_round6.md:40–44`) requires either an additional **hygiene-matched pre-`R_sleep` control** or restricting that isolated withholding attribution to parent MTZs already identical to the hygienic input (**future required verification, with no scale-invariance conclusion available now**).
   - **Preregistration prerequisites for `R_sleep`:** Exact `R_sleep` reflection membership/fraction and resolution-shell stratification, hygiene-matched pre-`R_sleep` control construction/eligibility, sealing mechanics, verification of exclusion from internal scaling/weight tuning, evaluator scoring rules, and leakage audits are mandatory prerequisites to specify in preregistration before any canary or trial run.
